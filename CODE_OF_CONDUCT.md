# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers, accept constructive feedback, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report violations privately through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/jscode-es/forma/security/advisories/new) on this repository. Maintainers may remove content and ban participants who violate this code.
